# Privacy Policy — FRDM+ for Android TV

_Last updated: November 7, 2026_

The FRDM+ Android TV app ("the app") shows the FRDM+ streaming service
(https://frdmplus.com, operated by No Brakes LLC) on Android TV and Google TV
devices. The app is open source; all of its code is in this repository.

## Data the app's own code collects

None. The app's code contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or
tracking libraries, and it doesn't send anything to the app's developer.

## Data collected through the FRDM+ service inside the app

Everything you do in the app happens on the FRDM+ website, which collects and
uses data under the FRDM+ Privacy Policy (https://frdmplus.com/readme/privacy).
When you use the app, that can include:

| Data | Examples | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Account info | Email address, name, password (sent encrypted to FRDM+) | Signing in and managing your account |
| Purchases | Subscription status | Unlocking subscriber content |
| App activity | Videos watched, watch history, favorites, searches | Resume watching, favorites, recommendations |
| Device or other IDs | A device ID the site stores, cookie identifiers | Keeping you signed in, security, analytics |
| Usage and diagnostics | Pages viewed, IP address, browser/device type | Operating and improving the service |

The FRDM+ website also uses third-party services that may receive some of this
data, including **Google Tag Manager / Google Analytics** and the **Meta (Facebook)
Pixel**, for analytics and marketing. All data is sent over encrypted (HTTPS)
connections.

## Data stored on your TV

Like a web browser, the app keeps the FRDM+ website's cookies, local storage and
cache on your TV so you stay signed in and pages load faster. This data isn't
backed up to the cloud by the app. To remove it, sign out in the app, uninstall
the app, or use **Settings → Apps → FRDM+ → Clear data**.

## Your choices

- **Deleting your account or data:** contact FRDM+ through the **Contact Us** link at
  https://frdmplus.com/settings/about to ask for your account and associated data
  to be deleted.
- Accounts can't be created inside the TV app; new accounts are created on the
  FRDM+ website.

## Permissions

- **Internet**: to load the FRDM+ service.
- **Protected media playback** is allowed so DRM-protected FRDM+ videos can play. Camera and microphone requests are always refused.

## Children

The app and the FRDM+ service are not directed to children under 13.

## Contact

Questions about this policy: use the **Contact Us** link at
https://frdmplus.com/settings/about, or open an issue in this repository.
