# Privacy Policy — FRDM+ for Android TV

_Last updated: November 7, 2026_

The FRDM+ Android TV app displays the FRDM+ website (https://frdmplus.com) on
your TV. It is open source; the full code is in this repository.

## What the app itself collects

Nothing. The app has no analytics, advertising, crash reporting or tracking
code, and it does not send data to the app's developer or anyone else.

## What is stored on your TV

Like any web browser, the app keeps the FRDM+ website's cookies, local storage
and cache on your device so you stay signed in and pages load faster. This data
stays on the TV. You can delete it by uninstalling the app or by clearing its
storage in **Settings → Apps → FRDM+ → Clear data**.

## The FRDM+ website

When you use FRDM+ inside the app, the website may collect information (for
example your account details, subscription and viewing activity) under its own
privacy policy, available on frdmplus.com. The app does not change or add to
what the website collects.

## Permissions

- **Internet / network state** — to load the website.
- **Protected media playback** — granted to the website only so DRM-protected
  videos can play. Camera and microphone requests are always refused.

## Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue in this repository.
